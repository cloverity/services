Optor Games bot — Simple

Simple bot — the minimum: games as a list of buttons under a banner, a game card with Play / Game page / Back. Admin picks a game from a button list to edit or delete it; adding is a step-by-step dialog. The bot is private: every team gets its own password.
View as
Admins: @Nik_Unic, @maksym1186, @C1overity. The bot recognises them by Telegram ID, shows them a different bottom menu and never asks them for a password. “New visitor” logs the subscriber out and starts their chat from scratch.
Team passwords (demo)
    Click a password to paste it into the message box, then press Send. Anything else is a wrong password. After “New password” the old one stops working for new people.
